3.0 - the same data on iPhone, iPad and Mac

What to try:
1. Install 3.0 on the iPhone first and open it once. Then install it on the Mac and the iPad and open it there.
2. On the phone, log a set at the gym. Watch it appear on the Mac within about a minute (the app open on both).
3. Change something on the Mac (a workout's name, a machine). See it on the phone.
4. Settings > Your data > iCloud: the dot and words (green "Same on all devices"), and the green Sync now button.
5. Settings > Backups > "From before iCloud": the copy each device kept before it first met iCloud.
6. On the iPad and the Mac: the side list on the left (Workouts, At the gym, History, Settings) and the iCloud dot at its foot.
7. iPhone only: the lock screen rest, the gym reminder, heart rate from Apple Health.
Not yet tried on a real iPad or Mac with iCloud. Please tell me what looks wrong.
